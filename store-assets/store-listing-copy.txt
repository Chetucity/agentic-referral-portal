FULL DESCRIPTION                                  (max 4,000 chars)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Job boards show you a listing and then silence. ReferIn shows you the people
inside the company who can actually put your name forward — and gives you a
status you can check, from the moment you ask to the moment you're hired.

WHY REFERRALS
A referred candidate is far more likely to reach an interview than someone who
applies cold. Most people know this. What they don't have is a way to find the
right person inside the company, ask them properly, and know what happened
next. That's the gap ReferIn closes.

FOR JOB SEEKERS
• Browse openings and filter by role, location and work mode
• See exactly who at that company has opted in to referring — their team, their
  title, how long they've been there, and how many referrals they've made
• Ask a named person directly, with a note explaining why you fit
• Build an ATS-scored resume inside the app and attach it to your request
• Track every referral through a visible pipeline: Requested → Accepted →
  Referred → Interviewing → Hired
• Get notified the moment anything changes. No more chasing.

FOR EMPLOYEES WHO REFER
• Turn on "open to refer" and appear on your company's openings
• Receive requests in a real inbox, with the candidate's note and resume
  already attached
• Accept or decline with a reason, then mark someone as formally referred
• See your own track record — how many you've put forward, how many were hired

FOR RECRUITERS
• Post, pause and close openings, with live seat counts
• See every referral into your company in one pipeline instead of across email
  threads and spreadsheets
• Move candidates through interview to hired or rejected
• Manage your company profile and the team who can refer into it

THE RESUME BUILDER
Build a resume without leaving the app. Score it against a rule-based ATS model
out of 100, get live writing feedback on weak phrasing and passive voice, match
it against a job description, and export to PDF, Word or PNG. Or upload an
existing resume and improve it. Everything you build is yours, private by
default, and attachable to a referral in one tap.

BUILT ON A CLEAR IDEA
Every status change is timestamped and written to a permanent history, so the
candidate, the referrer and the recruiter all read the same truth. A job seeker
can never move their own referral forward — that would defeat the point. What
you see is what actually happened.

PRIVACY
Your resume stays private until you attach it to a referral, and then only the
person you asked and that company's recruiters can read it. We don't sell your
data and we don't show ads. You can delete your account, and everything in it,
from inside the app at any time.

Free to use.
